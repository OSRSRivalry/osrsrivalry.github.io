Frequently Asked Questions

Why isn't my player showing up?

Stats are pulled from the official OSRS Hiscores. Players below rank 2,000,000 in overall may not appear. Make sure the username is spelled correctly including spaces.

I bought Pro but it shows as locked

Go to Settings → OSRS Rivalry Pro → Restore Purchases. If the issue persists, contact us with your Apple ID email.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings → Data. You can clear your activity feed, snapshots, and tracked players individually. All data is stored locally on your device — deleting the app removes everything.

Are notifications free?

Basic level-up notifications are available on the free tier. Inactivity alerts and the daily reminder require OSRS Rivalry Pro.

Is this app affiliated with Jagex?

No. OSRS Rivalry is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Jagex Ltd. Player data is sourced from the public OSRS Hiscores API.
