Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

OSRS Rivalry is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share your personal data on our servers. All data is stored locally on your device.

Data We Store Locally

The following data is stored on your device only:

  • Your RuneScape username
  • Usernames of players you choose to track
  • Your notification preferences
  • Progress snapshots you create
  • Rivalry and skill race records

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us.

Third-Party Services

OSRS Rivalry fetches publicly available player statistics from the Official Old School RuneScape Hiscores API, operated by Jagex Ltd. When you search for or track a player, their username is sent to this API to retrieve their stats. No other data is transmitted.

OSRS Rivalry is not affiliated with or endorsed by Jagex Ltd.

Notifications

All push notifications — level-ups, inactivity alerts, and daily reminders — are scheduled locally on your device using iOS notification APIs. No data is sent to external servers to deliver notifications.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. OSRS Rivalry does not receive or store your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs all payment processing.

Analytics & Advertising

OSRS Rivalry does not include any advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking frameworks. We do not track your behaviour within the app or sell your data to third parties.

Children's Privacy

OSRS Rivalry does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@osrsrivalry.app
