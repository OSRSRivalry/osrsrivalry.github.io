The ultimate companion app for Old School RuneScape players who want more than a stat checker.
TRACK ANY PLAYER
Search any OSRS player by username and follow their progress in real time. See every skill level, boss kill count, and hiscores rank — pulled live from the official Jagex hiscores.
RIVALRIES
Set up a head-to-head rivalry and compare every skill and boss kill count side by side. See who's pulling ahead, who's slacking, and where the real competition is.
SKILL RACES
Start a timed XP race in any skill. See who gains the most before the clock runs out.
LEADERBOARD
Rank your tracked players by total level, total XP, combat level, 99s, boss KC, or any individual skill.
PROGRESS SNAPSHOTS
Capture a player's stats at any moment and compare against future snapshots to track gains over days, weeks, or months.
SKILLING GUIDE
Training methods for every skill including Sailing — with XP rates, level requirements, and notes.
NOTIFICATIONS
Get alerted when a rival gains a level, when a tracked player goes quiet, or get a daily nudge to check your rivalries.
CHARACTER CARDS
Generate a shareable card showing all 23 skills in the classic OSRS grid layout — built for every account type including level 3 skillers, zerkers, and ironmen.
